● National Priority Executive Directive

Executive Order No. 82: Inter-Agency Digital Governance & Data Harmonization Framework

Directs all thirty-four (34) executive departments, attached bureaus, and constitutional commissions to synchronize national registries into the DiWA open public wire by October 2026 to ensure public accountability and eliminate bureaucratic delays.

STATUS: ENFORCED · TARGET: ALL LINE AGENCIES · AUDIT: COA-VERIFIED

Your data and public trust stays yours.

Every document published on DiWA carries an immutable cryptographic hash matched against agency public keys. Under Executive Order No. 2 (s. 2016) on Freedom of Information, all circulars, en banc resolutions, and annual audit reports are public property without copyright barriers or monetization paywalls.

Frequently asked questions

How frequently are agency feeds synchronized?

Official RSS and JSON webhook endpoints are polled every 60 seconds. High-priority advisories from NDRRMC, DOH, and COMELEC are pushed instantaneously via direct government gateway feeds.

How can journalists and civic developers consume the raw data?

The raw wire is available via public REST endpoints at https://diwa.gov.ph/api/v1/feed.json and standard RSS feeds per agency. No API key or rate limit tiering is required for public interest and academic monitoring.

Are documents on DiWA legally recognized?

Yes. All published circulars and orders correspond to official issuances published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines or certified by the head of the issuing department pursuant to Section 18 of Executive Order No. 292.

How can an agency integrate its public feed into DiWA?

Agency IT teams can register their automated dispatch endpoints through the DICT National Government Portal 3.0 Interoperability Working Group. See our developer documentation or contact interop@diwa.gov.ph.
